Bring one thought. See it three ways.

Three companions look at the same worry through three lenses: mooca (psychology), Hin the rock (Stoic philosophy) and Bua the lotus (Buddhism, in everyday words). You pick the one that speaks to you, go deeper with it, and in about two minutes leave with a card and a next step sized to how heavy things are.

1The opportunity

People feel "off" long before they'd ever book a psychologist.

Booking therapy feels like too much, and mental-health quizzes feel clinical. Many Thais already cope through the temple or through philosophy first. The gap isn't information. It's a small, low-stakes first step that feels like theirs.

Who

Thai working adults, 22–35

Busy and often tired. They would tap a link a friend shares, but would never search for "therapy". They have two minutes on the BTS, not twenty.

Why they'd start

One thought is already on their mind

The ask is tiny and concrete: bring one thought. No sign-up, no score, no diagnosis. Three cute companions make it feel like a game you can safely try.

The insight

You don't know which lens helps until you see a few

Some people want to reframe a thought, some want to act, some want to let it go. So Mindscape shows all three side by side and lets the person choose.

What they leave with
  • Understanding: their worry seen from a new angle, in their own words
  • A feeling: a little lighter, and less alone with it
  • Something to keep: one card to save or share
  • A next step: carry on with the same friend in ooca Garden, or an ooca psychologist when the signals say so
How we'd know it worked
  • Completion: door to card, without dropping off in the room
  • "Do you understand yourself a little better?" one tap at the end, no score
  • Cards saved or shared, and people coming back tomorrow
  • On the care tier: taps on "Talk to someone at ooca"

3Three rooms, one shape

Same four beats. One gesture that is the practice.

Name it → look closer → practise → step back. mooca changes the thought, Hin changes what you do about it, Bua changes nothing: you notice it, let it drift and pass some kindness on.

mooca

Psychology · CBT & ACT

Rewriting the forecast. Name the weather the thought brings, find what the worry says you care about, then soften the heavy words.

The act: tap to soften. Tap "everyone", "never", "not good enough" and swap each for a softer, truer word. Each swap names its thinking trap, and the sky clears a notch.

Name itThe weather this thought brings, plus a feeling word

Look closerThe silver lining: what the worry says you care about

PractiseTap a heavy word; pick a softer one; see the trap's name

Step backThe sky clears; on the card, the old thought crossed out

Hin

Stoic philosophy

A Stoic sitting. Separate what happened from the judgement laid on it, sort what's yours to move, choose a virtue, then see it from a year away.

The act: drag to sort, drag to rise. Sorting pebbles into "mine to move" and "let it be" is the dichotomy of control. Dragging up lifts the camera above the landscape.

Name itThe stone and the story: what actually happened

Look closerSplit the stone into pebbles you can and can't move

PractiseTurn "mine to move" into stepping stones, with a virtue

Step backRise above it: "a year from now, how much will this matter?"

Bua

Buddhism, in everyday words

Notice, let go, be kind. Find where the thought sits in the body, breathe with a lamp until the pond clears, then send one kind wish outward.

The act: hold. Holding still for five slow breaths is what settles the muddy water; holding again sends kindness in widening circles, out to Hin and then mooca.

Name itWhere you feel it, and what it's like there

Look closerLet it drift: five breaths with the lamp, and the note floats away

PractisePick a wish, hold to send it: me, someone I love, everyone

Step backKindness reaches everyone; nothing fixed, just noticed and let go

Built on the ooca CI and ooca's new mooca, with two friends drawn to match.

mooca · ooca's own

Kept as ooca drew it, made to float

The puffy cloud head, small white body and turquoise scarf stay as on ooca's sheet. It floats, as our mooca always has here: legs hanging loose, a slow bob with a slight sway, and a faint shadow on the hills far below.

Hin · repainted

Same pebble, new finish

One stone with little feet, its sprout and its worry crack, now in soft painted light with mooca's face: small oval eyes, a thin smile, puffed cheeks. At rest it breathes slowly and its sprout nods.

Bua · repainted

Same lotus, new finish

A bud on its own stem with a round leaf, petals still opening breath by breath in the lamp practice. At rest it sways on the water, its leaf a beat behind, and a slow ring spreads from its stem.

One face, every mood

The three share one face and one finish. mooca's weather shows in mooca itself: its face, its cloud's colour and the sky around it.

What we tried, and kept

Every option was built and compared in the scene itself before choosing.

Four more floating poses were tried for mooca: knees up, a cloud seat, a mochi with no body, and drifting. The chosen one keeps ooca's build.

  • Head on a body for Hin and Bua? Tried, set aside: they lost the shapes that say "stone" and "lotus".
  • A scarf for each friend? Kept for mooca only. On Hin it read as a belt; on Bua it crowded the bud.
  • Five backgrounds. The chosen one gives Bua's pond its own soft ground, so it no longer sits on Hin's sand.
  • Quiet motion, all still with reduced motion. A slow bob, a breath, a sway: alive, never busy.

The moment before the card

The friend you chose cheers inside a warm halo, the others smile along, and one line names what happened, in the room's voice. Then the note lifts out of the scene as it softly blurs away, and lands on its own words on the card as the card rises around it. A tap skips ahead.

8Safety, access & what's next

Approachable on the surface, careful underneath.

Safety

Help is never more than one tap away

  • "Need help now?" (1323, 1669, Samaritans) in the header on every screen
  • Crisis phrases checked in Thai and English; a match opens help and keeps a care note
  • "What's the point?" shows a gentle 1323 line
  • Nothing leaves the device; no account
Accessibility

Every practice works without a mouse

  • Pebbles: drag, or tap a pebble then a side
  • Lamp and kindness: hold Space or Enter
  • Focus moves to each step's heading; one h1 per screen
  • AA contrast; reduced motion turns off decorative movement
  • Checked at four phone sizes (360–430px): each step's answers show without scrolling, and follow-ups scroll into view
Stakeholders

Who I'd bring in before launch

  • Clinical team: all copy, crisis phrases, next-step thresholds
  • Mindfulness / Dhamma advisor: Bua's plain-language version
  • Native Thai writer: polish the Thai copy
  • Legal / PDPA: consent if it later links to booking
What I'd test first
  • Do people understand the three lenses from one line each, or do they always pick mooca?
  • Time per room in practice: is Bua's five-breath lamp too long for two minutes?
  • Does the care tier feel caring, or like being redirected?
What I left out, on purpose
  • Accounts, history and streaks: they belong in ooca Garden, not the free taste
  • A full assessment or any diagnosis language
  • Booking: the next step links out to ooca and 1323
